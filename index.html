Grammar · B1–B2

Passive voice
+ modal verbs

It can be learned. It should be practiced. It will be used.

Use the ← → keys, or swipe.

Warm-up

Look around you.

What could be improved here? What should be thrown away? What can’t be changed at all?

Reminder

Modal verbs

can
ability, possibility
She can drive.
could
past ability, possibility
It could rain later.
may / might
possibility
He might be late.
must
obligation, certainty
You must wear a seatbelt.
should
advice
You should call her.
will
future, prediction
Prices will go up.
have to
necessity
I have to work on Saturday.

Modal + base verb, with no to and no -s: not She cans drive or She can to drive.
Today: a modal + the passive → it can be done.

Think

Shoes must be removed
beyond this point

Why does the sign say this, and not “You must remove your shoes”? How does it feel different?

Form

modal + be + past participle

can
The bike can be repaired.
may / might
The flight might be delayed.
must
The form must be signed.
should
Plastic should be recycled.
will
The results will be announced tomorrow.
have to
The rent has to be paid by Friday.

NegativeBags must not be left here.

QuestionCan it be fixed?

Exercise 1

Complete the sentences.

Use the modal and the verb in parentheses.

  1. The form in black ink. (must / sign)
  2. Tickets online. (can / buy)
  3. This medicine after meals. (should / take)
  4. Food into the library. (must not / bring)
  5. in the washing machine? (can / this jacket / wash)

Exercise 2

Rewrite in the passive.

Leave out who does the action.

  1. You should water the plants every day.

  2. Someone will collect the trash on Monday.

  3. You must not leave bags unattended.

  4. We might change the date.

Your turn

Write the rules.

Choose a place — a library, a spaceship, a music festival, your kitchen. Write five rules for it.

Discuss

Should it be allowed?

  • Should uniforms be worn at school?
  • Should homework be abolished?
  • Should zoos be closed?
  • Should voting be made mandatory?
  • Should essays be written with AI?

Pick one. Argue for it — then argue against it.

Form · past

modal + have been + past participle

  • The window must have been broken last night.
  • The email should have been sent yesterday.
  • The letter might have been lost in the mail.
  • The accident could have been prevented.

Always have, never has: It must has been done.

Meaning

What are you saying?

must have been done
I’m almost sure it happened.
might / may / could have been done
Maybe it happened.
should have been done
It was the right thing to do — but it didn’t happen.
could have been done
It was possible — but it didn’t happen.

Notice: could have been can mean two things. Which one is it in “The accident could have been prevented”?

Exercise 3

Choose the best option.

The cake is gone, and the dog looks happy. It eaten by the dog.

The report is full of mistakes. It checked more carefully.

Nobody knows where the keys are. They left at the café.

Nobody checked the brakes. The accident prevented.

Think

What happened here?

You come home after a weekend away. The front door is open. The lights are on. The fridge is empty — but your laptop is still on the table. On the floor there is a note: “Sorry. — L.”

Give as many explanations as you can. Use the passive where it fits.

Reflect

In hindsight…

Think of something that went wrong — a trip, a project, a movie, a decision in your town. What should have been done differently? What could have been avoided?

Exercise 4

Find the mistake.

Each sentence has one mistake. Find it, then tap to check.

The report must be finish by Monday.

The report must be finished by Monday.

The problem should been solved years ago.

The problem should have been solved years ago.

Can be the problem fixed?

Can the problem be fixed?

The letter might has been lost.

The letter might have been lost.

Discuss

“Mistakes were made.”

  • “Mistakes may have been made.”
  • “Your data might have been accessed.”
  • “Difficult decisions had to be made.”

Who did it? Why don’t they say? When is the passive polite or useful — and when is it hiding something?

Summary

In one slide.

Present / future
modal + be + past participleIt must be done.
Past
modal + have been + past participleIt must have been done.
Negative
modal + not + be …It can’t be done.
Question
modal + subject + be …Can it be done?

Discuss · 1–6

Questions can be asked now.

  1. What can be seen from your bedroom window?
  2. Is your bed made every morning? Who is it made by?
  3. Which jobs at home are done by you? Which ones should be done by someone else?
  4. What can be done in your town on a rainy day?
  5. Have you ever been invited to a party you didn’t want to go to? What happened?
  6. What should be done with old clothes? Do you give them away, sell them, or throw them out?

Discuss · 7–12

And a few more.

  1. Which of your things should never be lent to anyone? Why?
  2. What music should be played at a wedding? What should never be played?
  3. Have you ever been given bad advice? What should you have been told?
  4. Which movie should be seen in a theater, not at home?
  5. What can be learned in one day? What can’t be learned in a year?
  6. What should be said when you arrive late to class? Has it ever happened to you?
