Then make a passive sentence with it.

Form

Only be changes.

present
Bread is made here every day.
past
This cake was made by my grandma.
present perfect
A mistake has been made.
future
Dinner will be made at seven.
modal
It can be made in ten minutes.

The past participle never changes: made, made, made.

Exercise 1

Complete the sentences.

Use the passive. Look at the time words.

  1. English in many countries. (speak)
  2. These photos by my grandfather in 1970. (take)
  3. My bike three times this year! (steal)
  4. The results to you tomorrow. (send)
  5. Too much food away every day. (throw)
  6. This bridge more than 100 years ago. (build)

Discuss

Your turn to talk.

  1. How often is your hair cut? Who is it cut by?
  2. What were you taught at school that you still use today?
  3. Have you ever been caught in the rain without an umbrella? What happened?
  4. What is sold at your local market? What do you usually buy there?
  5. Have you ever been taken somewhere as a surprise? Where?
  6. Is a lot of food thrown away in your home? What happens to leftovers?
  7. Have you ever been told you look like someone famous? Who?
  8. Have you ever been paid for doing something you love? What was it?
